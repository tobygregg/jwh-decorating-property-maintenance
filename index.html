What we do

Painting, decorating and upkeep, inside and out

Every job starts with proper preparation. Choose a service to see what's involved.

Interior decorating

Walls, ceilings and woodwork, prepared properly and finished cleanly. We sand with Festool kit and check every surface under site lighting before the top coat goes on.

What's included

  • Walls and ceilings
  • Doors, frames and skirtings
  • Panelling and feature woodwork
  • Filling, sanding and priming
Ask about interior work

Wallpapering

Feature walls and whole rooms, from lining paper to large patterned papers that need careful matching. Walls are prepared first so the paper sits flat and the joins disappear.

What's included

  • Wall preparation and lining
  • Patterned and feature papers
  • Pattern matching around doors and corners
  • Paint and paper together in one job
Ask about wallpapering

Exterior painting

Houses, shopfronts and commercial buildings painted to stand up to the island weather. Surfaces are cleaned, repaired and primed before any top coats go on.

What's included

  • Render and masonry
  • Windows, doors and frames
  • Shopfronts
  • Repairs and preparation before painting
Ask about exterior work

Spray finishing

Airless spraying gives an even finish with no brush or roller marks on doors, joinery and large areas. We use our own airless spray equipment.

What's included

  • Airless spraying
  • Doors and joinery
  • Large walls and ceilings
  • Masking and protection before we start
Ask about spraying

About JWH

Taking pride in every job

JWH is run by John Hutcheson, who leads a team of experienced decorators and works on the tools himself when needed. A good reputation built over the years has led to work on hotels, bars and period properties as well as homes.

The standard is the same on every job, large or small: proper preparation, clean lines and a finish that lasts.

  • Working from 7:30

    The team is on site and working at 7:30, Monday to Friday.

  • Deadlines kept

    We plan each job properly and haven't missed a deadline to date.

  • The right kit

    Festool sanders and site lighting, plus airless spray equipment.

  • One point of contact

    John is your contact from the first call to the final coat.
